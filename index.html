Who I am

I've spent my life moving between disciplines, places and ways of understanding the world — from computers and software to teaching, photography, independent projects and travel. Today I work across code, cameras and education, building software and practical tools, creating learning resources, and photographing people, streets and everyday life.

What connects it all is a desire to understand things firsthand, make something meaningful from what I discover, and share the result with others.

What I do

Open-Source Software

Developer Tools

Small, practical utilities: HTML minifiers, email templates, time-zone helpers, benchmarking tools and more.

Content & Education

Programming courses, books and open learning materials that make technical subjects approachable without losing the details that matter.

Photography

Travel, street, portrait and documentary work exploring people, places and everyday life.
