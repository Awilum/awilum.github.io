Who I am

I've spent my life moving between disciplines, places and ways of understanding the world — from computers and software to teaching, photography, independent projects and travel. Today I work across code, cameras and education, building software and practical tools, creating learning resources, and photographing people, streets and everyday life.

What connects it all is a desire to understand things firsthand, make something meaningful from what I discover, and share the result with others.

Awilum

Awilum is an ancient online name I've carried since the beginning of my IT career. Derived from the Akkadian awīlum, meaning "human being" or "person", it traces back to the ancient world of Mesopotamia.

Over the years, it has become more than a nickname. It represents my curiosity, independence, and fascination with exploring unfamiliar worlds, ideas, and possibilities. A small part of my own Wonderland.

This is the developer portfolio of Sergey Romanenko, featuring open-source libraries, tools, content-management systems, and the code behind the courses, photography, and publications on sergeyromanenko.com.
